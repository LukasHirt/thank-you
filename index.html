    About this sky

    Each star is a person who contributed to one of the public repositories of the owncloud and owncloud-docker organizations on GitHub. A star lights up on the day of that person’s first commit, pull request, review or issue. Its constellation is the project they worked on most.

    Brighter stars are people with code contributions: commits, pull requests and reviews. The fainter stardust is people who reported issues. Stars without a name belong to contributors whose commits aren’t linked to a GitHub account.

    Only public data is used. No email addresses are published. Automated accounts (bots) are left out. A few repositories began as copies of other open-source projects. For those, only the work done after they became part of ownCloud is counted.

    Don’t want to be shown? Open an issue or add your handle to optout.txt in the repository of this page, and your star will become anonymous with the next update of the page.
